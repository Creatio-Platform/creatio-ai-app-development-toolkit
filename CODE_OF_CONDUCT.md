# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Creatio AI App Development Toolkit project respectful, professional, and welcoming for contributors and users.

This applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, documentation, discussions, release channels, and direct communication related to the project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about technical risks and disagreements.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Accept maintainer decisions when they are made to protect project quality, safety, or scope.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing confidential information, credentials, logs with secrets, or private correspondence without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Personal attacks, insults, or hostile comments.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without explicit permission.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, customer data, or security-sensitive details in public project channels.
- Any conduct that maintainers reasonably determine to be inappropriate in a professional open source project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, issues, pull requests, or other content that does not follow this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior creates risk for contributors, users, the project, or Creatio-related ecosystems.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and based on the available context.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to:

**support@creatio.com**

If the report includes a security issue, follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not disclose security-sensitive details in public issues or pull requests.

Reports will be reviewed by project maintainers or the appropriate Creatio support channel. Information will be handled with appropriate care and shared only with people who need it to review and respond to the report.
