### What it does
The Employees section keeps the HR record for each employee - who they are (Contact, Full name), where they sit (Account, Org structure unit, Job, Full job title, Manager, Owner), their employment dates (career start/due, probation due, reason for dismissal) and notes. From the record you reach the employee career history, the contact's communication options, addresses, important events and career, plus their organizational and functional roles and attachments.

1. **BLOCKER for the form page / mini page / 3 child pages — the Layout tables are empty because the engine only
   recognises a Classic diff item as a FIELD when it carries an explicit `bindTo`** (`engine.mjs`: `eff.fields =
   alive.filter(i => i.bindTo)`). `EmployeePage`, `EmployeeMiniPage`, `EmployeeCareerPage`,
   `ContactAnniversaryPageV2` and `SysUserInRoleInUserPageV2` bind their columns the other standard Classic way —
   `{"operation":"insert","parentName":"ProfileContainer","name":"Name","values":{layout:…}}`, i.e. **implicit

   instead of mapped rows, and the Overview reads `0 fields`.
   The columns are real and confirmed on the entity (`get-entity-schema-properties`, merged view): `Name`,

   are shown on the Classic page but are NOT `Employee` columns — they come from the linked `Contact`, so on
   Freedom they need lookup-path attributes (`Contact.<column>`, `ForwardReference`) + read-only fields.

   their fields come from the parent template, which does use `bindTo`.
   **This is not an input gap** — the manifest carries the full layer chain, the 16-body parent-template seed,
   `entityColumns`, `columnTitles` and `resources`, and both engine gates are green (`--plan` exit 0).
