Status, health and priority get colored values (chips) shown on the record page and in lists, and the answer assigns colors by meaning, not by palette order: Red health is red, Green health and Completed are green, Amber health and On hold are amber or another attention color, Draft is neutral. A routine closing value such as Cancelled is neutral or muted rather than an alarming red. Text stays in the chip. A lookup with no state (client, project manager) is not colored. An answer that leaves these lookups plain, or colors them in an order unrelated to meaning, fails.
